01 / The observer

Football
is culture.

Independent player identification and talent evaluation, built around close observation, context and long-term potential.

Beyond the
ninety minutes.Matchday / Memory / Observation
Campania, Italy

I work as an independent football scout from Campania, Italy, focusing on identifying emerging players and evaluating how their qualities could translate to different levels and roles. My scouting combines live match observation in my local area with video analysis of players and competitions abroad.

I pay particular attention to technical quality, first touch, ball control, acceleration and changes of pace, but also to the intelligence behind each action: scanning, body orientation, timing, decisions under pressure and movement without the ball. Physical qualities matter, but I look first at how a player understands and influences the game.

My process moves from context to evidence: understanding the player's team and role, reviewing full-match footage where available, studying relevant statistics, comparing actions across different matches and building a clear assessment of strengths, limitations, positional fit and development potential.
