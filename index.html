Football
is culture.

The feeling of a ground. The rhythm of a player. The split-second decision most people miss. Scouting begins with looking closer.

I am an independent football scout based in Campania, Italy. My work combines live observation in my local area with video-based research across other markets. I look beyond the immediate performance to understand how technical qualities, decisions and movement may translate to a higher level.

01 / Technique

Technical quality

First touch, ball control, passing execution, carrying and performance under pressure.

02 / Perception

Game intelligence

Scanning, awareness, timing, decision-making and understanding of space.

03 / Movement

Dynamic tools

Acceleration, change of pace, agility and the ability to create or exploit separation.

04 / Role

Versatility

Role suitability, positional adaptability and contribution in different phases of play.

05 / Future

Projection

Development indicators, strengths to build on, limitations and realistic areas for growth.

06 / Evidence

Context

Age, competitive level, role, team environment and the evidence available for evaluation.
