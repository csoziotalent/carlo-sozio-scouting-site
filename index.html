01 / About

An independent
eye on the game.

I am an independent football scout based in Campania, Italy, focused on identifying and evaluating emerging talent. My work combines live observation in my local area with video-based research across other markets. I look beyond the immediate performance to understand how a player's technical qualities, decisions and movement may translate to a higher level.

02 / Methodology

What I look
for in a player.

A structured assessment built around the relationship between technique, perception, movement and context — not physical attributes in isolation.

01

Technical quality

First touch, ball control, passing execution, carrying and ability to perform under pressure.

02

Game intelligence

Scanning, awareness, timing, decision-making and understanding of space.

03

Dynamic tools

Acceleration, change of pace, agility and the ability to create or exploit separation.

04

Versatility

Role suitability, positional adaptability and contribution in different phases of play.

05

Projection

Development indicators, strengths to build on, limitations and realistic areas for growth.

06

Context

Age, competitive level, role, team environment and the evidence available for evaluation.
